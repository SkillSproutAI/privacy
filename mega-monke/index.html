Privacy Policy for Mega Monke

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Mega Monke is provided by SkillSprout AI, LLC. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Nothing leaves your device. Mega Monke has no network access of any kind. It cannot send data anywhere, because it never connects to anything.

No accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no profile. The game starts the moment you open it.

No tracking. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software of any kind.

What is saved. Three values are stored on your device so your game is where you left it: the monkey's weight, your tap count, and whether sound is muted. That is the entire list. Deleting the app deletes them.

No personal information is collected. Not your name, not your location, not your contacts, not an advertising identifier. The app never asks, and has no way to send it if it did.

Children

Mega Monke is suitable for all ages and is designed to be safe for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There are no advertisements, no in-app purchases, no links out of the app, and no way for players to communicate with one another.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
